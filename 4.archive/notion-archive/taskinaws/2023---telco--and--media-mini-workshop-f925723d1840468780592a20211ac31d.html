2023 Plan and Strategy

Goals from Francessca (WWCS VP Tech)

Customer ObsessionWe will continue to obsess over our customers. Being customer obsessed means that we listen to our customers so we can build on their behalf. Get in front of customers - whether that be in-person or virtually. If you can, visit their offices to see their day-to-day operations and really get to understand their culture, values, and goals. I’ll be doing just that as I kick off my annual_“50 customers, 50 countries, 50 days”_ tour. First up – kicking off 2023 with our teams across India and our official Hyderabad Region Launch – our 30th region.

Resilience and Operational Excellence2022 was a big year for our Resilience efforts, with the launch of the Resilience TFC, Resilience Core, Resilience Days, School of Resilience, our Single-Availability-Zone Health Check campaign, and Resilience trainings, as well as some pretty incredible thought leadership such as a feature in the Wall Street Journal by James Hamilton, AWS Multi-Region Fundamentals,  and theAWS Fault Isolation Boundaries whitepaper. We will scale these Resilience efforts in 2023. It is everyone’s responsibility to play a more proactive role in identifying customer patterns and highlighting areas of weakness. This is a Sales Marketing Global Services-wide focus – and we’ll continue with an AWS SMGS- wide goal in 2023.

AWS Digital Sovereignty PledgeToday, having control over digital assets, or digital sovereignty is more important than ever. In November 2022 we introduced the AWS Digital Sovereignty Pledge – our commitment to offering all AWS customers the most advanced set of sovereignty controls and features available in the cloud. Our approach to delivering on this pledge is to continue to make the AWS Cloud sovereign-by-design. Security and data privacy are our top priorities. Please review our pledge and let’s earn trust through transparent customer communication.  AWS Digital Sovereignty Pledge (English and French).

Security and Data ProtectionCustomer trust is the foundation of our business. One way we earn customer trust is by providing customers the services, tools, and resources required to protect their data. Listen closely to customers to understand their unique data protection needs. Check out this Data Privacy FAQ, Data Protection Cheat Sheet, and the Security Pillar of the AWS Well-Architected Frameworkto learn more. And when in doubt, escalate (early and high).**Efficiency and optimization (internally and for our customers)**Efficiency remains critical right now for our business and we must work smarter to actively avoid falling into Day 2 stasis.Architecture and workload optimization is top of mind for us at AWS because it’s important to our customers as we navigate the current macro-economic climate. In 2023 – we have plans to launch a key 2022 experiment known as Project Telemetryto over 250 customers globally. Let’s continue to look around corners to help customers save money, and do the same for our team internally. SustainabilityWe know that Sustainability will remain a top priority for the C-Suite, and for many customers as they need to understand and report on their impact on the environment. I encourage you to become familiar with the Carbon Footprint Tool, ourEnvironmental and Sustainability TFC, the Sustainability Pillar of the AWS Well-Architected Framework. Moreover, it’s important that we all know Amazon’s Climate Pledge commitment. Community, Volunteering, and Giving Back 2023 will bring many opportunities for giving back to our local and global communities. Be on the lookout for more information about our Community Engagement initiatives, our grassroots Corporate Social Responsibility initiatives in your local offices, and other ways to give back.  We spotlight these stories in our quarterly All-Hands meetings to not only congratulate our Builders for their contributions, but also to share best practices so these successful initiatives can be scaled globally, and lastly, to inspire others and show that no act of giving back is too small. Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility.

**People and Leadership Development (Being Earths Best Employer)**The leadership team and I are committed to building the world’s most diverse, inclusive, and equitable business and technology organization. Developing yourself and your teams allows us to innovate on behalf of our customers. One of our key strategic priorities is strengthening our organization capability and role-modeling our Amazonian culture. In 2023, we’ll be experimenting with more goals aimed at people and talent development including our Day 1 Manager Onboarding, Future Managers, Early Career Talent, Inclusion Ambassadors, Executive Coaching, Expat and Rotational assignments, Leading with Empathy and with our Leadership Principles.

2023 Strategic Imperatives and GoalsOur 4 key Strategic Imperatives remain for 2023: (1) Rapidly Acquire New Customers, (2) Accelerate Migrations to the Cloud, (3) Win New Workloads Through Digital Transformation, and (4) Strengthen our Organizational Capabilities. Be on the lookout for more information about our WWCS Tech Org 2023 goals in the coming weeks.

Korea

2022 Reflection

We are still closing the full year financials; however, Korea CS will most likely deliver **1,557M(96.1346.5M, 103.5% of plan, +7.5% YoY), 2/ Outstanding FSI performance (40.7M,148.51B), Samsung (770M),SKTITC(250M), LG CNS ($224.5M) and total 36 EDPs were signed, 4/ Migration Wins (84 wins, 125% of plan, 75% YoY). However, we had some growth challenges in Coupang/ISV and sales discipline issue.

2023 Financial Plan…2026 Vision

We are still in the process of finalizing Area level target by segments; however, our ambition is to grow Korea CS from a 1.557Bbusinessin2022toa==∗∗4.0B by 2026**== (28.7% CAGR - compound annual grouth rate, 31.0% of TAS - target account selling). To achieve this, 1/ Our vertically aligned sales teams will confidently engage the C-Suite with strong industry insight, 2/ Provoke strategic conversations with customer’s business personas ensuring that AWS is the right choice for their business transformation, 3/ Proactively discover hidden stars that require our partnership with particularly active developer engagement activities. Our vision is “Aspire to be the most innovative organization driving sustainable growth, expanding to new market, go global and helping customers build long term success with clearly differentiated AWS value propositions and industry insights”.

Organization

Korea CS Enterprise will evolve from current 5 sub-segment (Samsung, LG, SK, ENT IV, FSI) to 3 sub-segment model (ENT, Telco & MNE, FSI) in order to 1/ Align with WW IBU and BDSI structure, 2/ Exit from Chaebol focused GTM to purely IV motions, and 3/ Simplify overall ENT organization. With this change, we aim to achieve 1/ Better use of resources/use cases and build community within the IV, 2/ Easy to leverage WW/APJ SMEs (Subject Matter Experts) and BDs. ENT GF will be led by STL (Single Threaded Leader) reporting to ENT head.

DNB/Game/ISV and SMB organization will remain unchanged.

With this change, ENT will be led by Younsik Kim, Telco & MNE will be led by Jaewon Shin, FSI will continue to be led by Kung Fun No, DNB/Game/ISV by JuHyeon Song, SMB by Chulwoo Park, CSC by Soo Chang Eom, SA by KiWaon Kim, BD by HyunSuk Lee, WWSO by Kyungku Seo, SSOE by Pill Sim Jeong, and SUP by Do Yeon Kim (reporting to APJ). During this transition, Doo Hyon Hong decided to pursue his career outside of AWS and his last day will be Jan 31st. He has been with AWS for more than 7 years and he’s been instrumental to build the current Enterprise IV team. Please join me in wishing Doo Hyon all the best in his next chapter.

Conclusion

To deliver the plan in 2023, we will execute the FY23 strategies and initiatives: 1/ Continue the growth momentum in Enterprise, DNB other than Coupang, and SMB, 2/ Mobilize new Enterprise structure (FSI / Telco & MNE / ENT) to build foundation for the next 3 years, 3/ Rapidly rebound Coupang business with focused approach and diversified account coverage beyond e-Commerce, 4/ Successfully implement management of change under new HC guidance and focus on productivity improvement and efficient resource allocation.
