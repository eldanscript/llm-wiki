Use Case Example

Modern Data Architecture Usecase

Customer Background

Octank baby develop science-based products, content and services to enhance child well-being and to help solve everyday parenting challenges. Their main product is a smart baby bed which provides womb-like rumbly sound and gentle rocking. The smart bed detects body movement and fussing and responds with more motion and sound, which can calm the baby in under a minute unless they are hungry or uncomfortable.

This customer wants to build their data analytics infrastructure from scratch. They are asking for a reference architecture from you. The reference architecture needs to support internal and external data sources, can scale with their business growth and is extendable to ML model training.

Business Use case

  1. The company wants to improve customer experience with new features and better support. They want to make data-driven decisions from various data sources like sales, support and social media.

  2. The company wants to add ML based new features like image recognition and pattern recognition. These new features will improve their market competitiveness.

  3. The  company wants to build a serverless solution with fully managed AWS  services.

Data Consumers

  • Business analysts

    • Number of users: 20 data analysts

    • Multi-tenancy: Not needed

    • Tool: Currently is using Tableau for some basic excel/csv file-based reporting, open to other BI tools.

    • How real time do you need the data: Daily

    • Insights: sales trends, loved features, features needed to improve

  • Data Engineer

    • Number of users: 10 data engineers

    • Multi-tenancy: Not needed

    • Tool: Any SQL interface

    • How real time do you need the data: As soon as the data is in the pipeline.

    • Insights: data quality check, transformation correctness check

  • Data scientists

    • Number of users: 5 data scientists, plan to grow 3x.

    • Multi-tenancy: Not needed

    • Tool: Any notebook env.

    • How real time do you need the data: Daily for model training. Real-time for model inference

    • Insights:  Image recognition, movement detection and anomaly detection.

Data Sources

  1. Application Data: Customer info, account metadata is stored in a MySQL database. 10MB new data per hour. Want to ingest the data continuously.

  2. Orders Data: Data about customer orders details is sourced from Shopify. 10-100 MB per hour. JSON format. Want to ingest the data every 10 min at minimum.

  3. Customer support data: Customer support data is sourced from Zendesk. 200 MB per JSON format. Want to ingest the data every 10 min at minimum.

  4. Third party data: Third party data from sites like Facebook. 1-10GB per day. Semi-structured Want to ingest the data every 10 min at minimum.

Device data: SNOO Device logs for now. Plan to support image and video streams in the future. These two data sources need to be streamed in continuously.

APJ Analytics Go Big Question Bank

Day 1 - Session 1 : Data Discovery

Question Bank

Define the Opportunity

  • What insights are you getting from the data?

  • How would getting insight into data provide value to the business?

  • Are you looking to create new revenue stream from your data?

  • What are challenges with your current approach/tool?

  • What are you not providing to customers that you would like to provide?

  • Who is the executive level stakeholder for this effort?

  • Example specific use case questions:

  • How does data define your customer acquisition strategy?

  • Would your business benefit from exploring modern approaches to [managing
    fraud detection, predictive maintenance, customer 360, IoT,
    clickstream, operational analytics, root-cause analysis to reduce mean
    time to detection and mean time to recovery]?

  • How are you continuously innovating on behalf of your customers and improving their user experience?

Technical Questions

Step 1 - Data Consumers + Consumption Layer

Common data consumers are business analysts, data engineer, data scientists.

  • Who are the users?

  • What Insights are you currently getting from your data?

  • What insights are on your roadmap?

  • Do you have a multi-tenant data model?

  • What are the different consumption models?

  • Which tool/interface do consumers use?

  • How real time does this use case need (i.e. near real time, 15 min, daily)?

  • What is the total number of consumers for this consumption model?

  • What is the peak concurrency?

Step 2 - Data Sources / Ingestion

  • How many data sources do you need to support?

  • Where/How is the data generated?

  • What are the different types of your data? (Structured, semi-structured, non-structured)

  • What are the different formats of your data? (JSON, CSV, FHIR)

  • Is your data originating from on-prem, third-party vendor, or in the cloud?

  • Is this data source streaming, batch, or micro-batch?

  • Rate and volume of ingestion

  • What is the ingestion interface (API, sftp, s3, marketplace)

  • How do your team on-board new data sources?

Step 3 - Storage Layer (Catalog + Storage) - Storage, Repo, Security, Compliance, Access

  • What data stores do you have?

  • What is the purpose of each data store?

  • Why that storage method (files, SQL, no-SQL, data warehouse)?

  • How do you currently organize your data? (Data Tiering, Partition)

  • How much data are you storing now, and how much do you expect to be storing 18 months from now?

  • How do you manage data governance?

  • What data regulatory and governance compliance do you face?

  • What is your DR strategy?

Step 4 - Processing Layer

  • Do you need to transform or enrich the data before end users consume the data?

  • What tools do you currently use for transforming your data?

  • Do you have a visual editor for the transformation code?

  • What is your frequency of data transformation (realtime, micro-batching, overnight batch)?

  • Is there any constraint with your current tool of choice?

Cloud Analytics Adoption Framework General Questions

  • What stage of adoption or evaluation is this?

  • What is the timeline of your project?

  • Do you have adequate staffing and skills to implement your data strategy?

  • Are you open to use ProServe and/or partner?

  • Have you identified a budget? What is it?

  • Do you have a preference for particular platforms/technologies?

  • 고객의 경험을 증대시키고 고객들을 대신해서 어떤 혁신을 계속하고 있습니까?

Technical Questions

Step 1 - Data Consumers + Consumption Layer

Common data consumers are business analysts, data engineer, data scientists.

  • 최종 사용자가 누구인가요?

  • 지금은 데이터를 이용해서 어떤 인사이트를 얻고 있나요?

  • 최종적으로 데이터로부터 얻고 싶은 인사이트는 무엇인가요?

  • 멀티 테넌트 데이터 모델을 현재 사용하고 있나요?

  • 데이터를 소비하는 컨슈머 쪽은 어떻게 구성되어 있나요?

  • 컨슈머가 어떤 툴이나 인터페이스를 사용하고 있나요?

  • 데이터 실시간성이 어느정도 필요한가요? (준실시간, 15분, 데일리)?

  • 최종적으로 데이터를 소비하는 컨슈머의 갯수는 얼마나 되나요?

  • 최대 동시 사용은 얼마로 예상하고 계신가요?

Step 2 - Data Sources / Ingestion

  • 데이터 소스는 몇 개나 연결하실 예정인가요?

  • 데이터가 앞단에서 어디서/어떻게 생성되나요?

  • 데이터 타입은 어떠한가요? (정형, 반정형, 비정형)

  • 데이터 포멧은 어떠한가요? (JSON, CSV, FHIR)

  • 데이터가 어디서부터 오나요? 온프렘? 서드파티 벤더? 클라우드?

  • 데이터가 스트리밍으로 오나요? 아니면 배치나 마이크로 배치?

  • 수집하는 데이터 전체 사이즈와 (단위 시간당, 또는 일당 등) 비율은 어떻게 될까요? (예: 1분에 10메가씩 수집)

  • 수집하고 있는 인터페이스는 어떤것들이 있나요? (API, sftp, s3, marketplace)

  • 신규 데이터소스가 생기면 어떻게 추가하고 있나요?

Step 3 - Storage Layer (Catalog + Storage) - Storage, Repo, Security, Compliance, Access

  • 어떤 데이터 저장소를 사용하고 있나요?

  • (데이터 저장소가 여러개라면) 각 데이터 저장소의 목적이 각각 다른가요? 다르다면 어떻게 다른가요?

  • (데이터를 특정방식으로 저장한다면) 왜 그런 방식으로 저장하고 있나요(files, SQL, no-SQL, data warehouse)?

  • 데이터를 어떤식으로 체계화(정리)하고 있나요? (Data Tiering, Partition)

  • 지금 저장하고 있는 데이터 용량은 얼마나 되나요? 그리고 지금으로부터 18개월 이후에는 얼마 더 커질 것으로 예상하시나요?

  • 데이터 거버넌스는 어떻게 관리하고 계신가요

  • 데이터와 관련해서 현재 직면하고 있는 규제사항들이 있나요?

  • 재해복구(DR) 전략이 있으신가요? 있다면 어떤 것인가요?

Step 4 - Processing Layer

  • 사용자가 데이터를 소비하기 전에 데이터 전처리가 필요하신가요?

  • 어떤 툴로 데이터를 변환하고 계신가요

  • 전처리 코드를 만들기 위한 시각화 에디터를 사용하고 계신가요?

  • 데이터 전처리 주기는 어떻게 되나요? (realtime, micro-batching, overnight batch)현재 사용하는 툴의 제약사항이나 문제점이 있나요?

Cloud Analytics Adoption Framework General Questions

  • 도입이나 평가 단계일텐데 어떤 단계쯤 인가요?

  • 프로젝트의 타임라인이 언제까지 인가요?

  • 생각하고 있는 데이터 전략을 수행하기 위한 인력이나 스킬셋이 충분히 있는 상태인가요?

  • 프로서브나 파트너를 이용하실 수도 있나요?

  • 예산 규모가 정해졌나요? 그렇다면 어느정도 인가요?

  • 선호하는 플랫폼이나 기술이 있나요
