Interview Questions

Intro

  • Give me a 1 minute run down of your background and how you evolved into the role you’re in today?

  • What do you understand this role to be, and why does that interest you?

App Dev

  • What are some examples of web application frameworks?

    • Of these are you experienced in any of them, tell me about them, what are the pros/cons?

  • If you were to design a distributed and scalable system, what are the key issues to consider in this design and why?

  • Can you talk to me about how you optimize your code for performance?

    • What tools or techniques do you use?

  • Do you have any experience with web or application servers?

    • Tell me some ways you’d stop/start or configure a webserver.

  • Explain how development changes get moved into production – or – What is Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) in the software development process?

  • What are some considerations when building an application for both desktop and mobile users?

  • What are some types of version control systems?

    • How do version control systems work?

  • What is a microservices design and what are its benefits? Are there any drawbacks?

    • What is the advantage or purpose of this design?

  • What is a queue? What is an example use case?

    • What is an example use case for a FIFO?

  • What is continuous integration?

    • Why would you want to use continuous integration?

    • What are some of the tools available for use in a CI workflow?

  • What is JSON commonly used for and what shortcomings of other languages does it address?

    • For XML?

  • What is the purpose of a Publish and Subscribe pattern and what would be a good case to use it?

  • What is the purpose of a thread pool?

  • What steps would you take to troubleshoot a website error of “Error establishing connection to database?

  • What techniques do you use to insure that your code has a low bug rate and performs well under load?

  • What’s a three-tiered web application?

    • How would you architect a three-tiered web application to limit downtime?

    • Why do people use multi-tier designs?

  • What’s the difference between a scripting language and a compiled language? Can you tell us the pro and cons of each?

    • What are some examples of both?

    • When would you choose one over the other?

  • When you are trying to build a high-volume distributed web application, what are some of the key areas you have to be concerned with?

    • How would you architect your application to take advantage of large scale distribution?

Networking

  • Can you explain the difference between Recovery Point Objective and Recovery Time Objective?

  • Can you talk about any layers of the OSI model?

    • Can you give examples of what operates at each layer?

  • Describe the difference between unicast and multicast. Can you explain when you would use unicast vs multicast transmission?

  • How does the process of translating a hostname to an IP address work?

  • What are ephemeral ports?

  • What are three different network routing protocols?

    • How do these protocols differ?

  • What is a CIDR block?

  • What is a virtual interface?

  • What is an MPLS network?

  • What is the difference between load balancing at layer 4 and load balancing at layer 7?

  • What’s the difference between a layer 2 and layer 3 network?

  • You are designing an application that is going to deliver real-time media. Would you choose TCP or UDP for your transport layer? Why?

    • What are the differences?

    • What are use cases for each and why?

  • You are in the process of migrating data centers. You’re seeing poor throughput when transfering a lot of data from one server in one data center to another server across the country. You’ve got a high bandwidth and high latency link between the two. What steps could you take to improve the throughput?

  • You just deployed an virtual machine in the cloud. You configure your instance to be a webserver. You know you can access the internet from the instance as you downloaded the necessary packages. However, when trying to access your webpage, you receive an error message. How would you troubleshoot?

Infrastructure

  • Can you explain to me the concept of containerization?

  • Have you ever used Infrastructure/System Monitoring tools?

    • Of these tools, what are the advantages and disadvantages?

    • What are the advantages of a comprehensive system monitoring framework?

  • In a datacenter/on-premise environment: what are the minimum requirements for making a website available on the internet?

  • You’ve been tasked with identifying the level of efficiency of hardware use in your datacenters, how would you approach this?

Content Delivery

  • Can you talk about the importance of internationalization or localization when delivering content to your end users?

  • Can you think of an example of when you’d need to pass query-string parameters to a CDN?

  • How would you help a customer with short life span content that needs to be massively distributed globally?

  • Imagine your employees are spread worldwide. What methods might you employ to make sure only authorized employees could get content from your CDN?

  • What is a CDN?

    • When would you use a CDN?

  • Without using caching, what are some ways to speed up delivery of assets to end users from a web server?

Storage

  • I have a database on a physical server that needs more disk space. Walk me through the steps you’d take to add storage capacity to the server to make it useable for the database. Assume you have all necessary assets on hand. Use whatever OS you’d like….

  • I have an application on a server that is I/O bound (i.e. I/O is the bottleneck.) What can I do to add more I/O capacity to the server?

  • What are SAN, NAS, and DAS?

  • What does RAID mean?

    • What are some common RAID types?

    • What purpose do these RAID types serve best?

  • What is data deduplication and why is it important?

    • What are some ways you can do data deduplication?

  • What is the difference between block storage and object storage?

  • What is the difference between durability and availability?

  • What’s the difference between hardware and software RAID?

Big Data / Analytics

  • Give me an example of how you might use analytics to enhance a product or bring benefit to your company.

  • Imagine a big stream of data. Can you talk about the lifecycle of working with a big data stream?

  • Imagine you have a large log file showing visitors to your customer facing website. What methods would you use to filter out all entries coming from within your company, or to view only visits coming from outside your company?

  • Let us say that you need to provide real-time analysis of a microblogging/social network platform (e.g. people unhappy vs. people happy about a product announcement). What would be the technology components you would use?

    • How would you analyze the results?

    • What type of noise might you have to filter out?

  • What is HDFS?

    • Why does it exist?

    • What are good use cases for HDFS?

    • What is the advantage it provides for its use-case?

  • What is MapReduce?

    • Can you describe what a mapper function or reducer function might do?

    • What are some common tools to use for map reduce operations?

  • When someone says “Big Data,” what does that mean to you? Can you provide examples?

  • Why are NoSQL databases usually thought of as a better alternative for big data applications compared to traditional relational databases?

  • Why is JSON an attractive protocol for data encapsulation or moving data between loosely coupled systems?

Security

  • How are certificates used to validate the authenticity of a server?

  • How would you store encrypted keys in the cloud?

  • In cryptography, what is the difference between symmetric algorithms and asymmetric algorithms?

  • Tell me a few ways passwords or credentials can be compromised, and how you would prevent it.

  • What are other ways you can secure login access besides a password?

  • What does it mean to federate an identity?

  • What is the design intent of antivirus software?

  • What is the difference between a stateful and a stateless firewall?

  • What is the difference between Authentication and Authorization?

  • What is TLS mutual authentication?

  • What’a a WAF?

    • What’s its relationship to a firewall?

  • What’s a firewall and how would you use it to secure your network?

  • What’s the difference between IDS and IPS?

  • Why do organization use a bastion host or jump box?

  • Why should customers use MFA - multi-factor authentication?

  • Why would you use file hashing?

Performance / Tuning

  • How can you identify network latency?

    • How would you go about reducing it in a local network or on the internet?

  • How would you describe cache?

    • What are some pros and cons of using cache?

  • I run a web site, my users are complaining it is slow. I just hired you as my consultant, walk me through troubleshooting my site.

  • What is a Denial of Service attack?

  • What is a GPU?

    • What purposes do GPUs have?

    • What is the advantage a GPU can offer and in what use-cases?

  • What is the difference between swapping and paging?

    • What are the performance impacts of either?

Database

  • How do you design a database for high-availability?

  • Security of data is always important within a database, discuss for me the layers of security that are available and the level of protection they represent.

  • What are indexes in a database?

    • What types of data are good candidates for indexes?

    • What types of data are bad candidates for indexes?

  • What are some types of SQL databases?

    • Can you give me an example of a database query to select users?

  • What are the ways in which you would scale a database?

  • What does it mean to denormalize a table and why would it be done?

  • What is the difference between a NoSQL database and SQL database?

    • What are some types of NoSQL databases?

  • What kind of tools would you use in order to diagnose a slow running query?

    • What is the information those tools provide that is significant?

  • What role does a caching service perform when referring to databases?

    • Can you name examples of products?

    • Would benefits be felt for both reads and writes?

    • When should one not use cache?

Systems

  • Imagine you are responsible for ensuring that a particular blog post on your company’s site could handle an extreme amount of traffic when it was published, how would you handle the preparation for that traffic?

  • In a scenario where you’re building out a three-tiered web application (a front end, application layer, and database layer), what would you do to eliminate single points of failure?

    • If a page in the app was slow to load, what would you do to troubleshoot or fix it?

Architecture

  • Assume you are tasked with deploying a new global web application that will start with ~1000 users, , but may scale to millions of users. There are periods of low and high utilization. What considerations would you take into account when you are building the infrastructure?

  • How would you design a web application if you had to focus on the greatest level of availability and scalability?

  • You have traced a performance issue with your website to the database component of the infrastructure, what architectural changes would you make to address this and how would they help

Compute / Serverless

  • Could you give me an example of a complex OS issue you had to resolve? What was the problem, how did you diagnose it and resolve it?

  • You are trying to ssh to a system. After you run the “ssh” command, it takes more than 10 seconds to get to the destination system to either provide you the login prompt (if enabled) or into the system. How would you troubleshoot it?

  • You have traced a performance issue with your website to the database component of the infrastructure, what architectural changes would you make to address this and how would they help

Networking / Connect

  • What can you tell me about the various layers of the OSI model? Where do TCP and IP map into the OSI model? Optional Followup: Why would knowledge of the OSI model be important?

  • Can you tell me about IP / CIDR calculation or perhaps a time where you needed to design an IP structure for an organisation, how did you go about it?

    • Where would you use a /30 CIDR and how would you calculate how the network and usable host addresses available?

  • Explain in as much detail as you can: what happens behind the scenes when I navigate to a http URL with a browser? What are the different components or services that the request will hit or use before reaching a web server?

    • What happens when the request is https?

  • Can you tell me about a time where you needed to implement NAT and PAT? How would they would be used in an enterprise organisation?

  • What can you tell me about TLS and mutual TLS? Where would symmetric and asymmetric encryption be used in TLS?

  • Tell me about a time when you had a critical customer (major external customer, Sr manager, CEO, etc.) complain that your network as a whole was running too slowly. How did you get to the root cause of the complaint? Optional followup: What were some of the tools and commands you used to gather the details needed to reach that conclusion?

  • What is the difference between TCP and UDP protocols?

Storage / CDN

  • You are assigned a project to set up a live video streaming for a rare-solar eclipse event, how would you do that?

  • You are supporting a customer and there is a concern around IO bottlenecks and durability for a new workload. How would you approach this scenario and figure out what to implement?

  • What are the architectural differences between SAN and NAS? What are the most appropriate use cases for each?

  • What is data de-duplication and why is it important?

  • Could you give me an example of a hierarchical storage or backup system? Can you provide examples of its use?

  • When should we consider using a Content Delivery Network? How does it affect the end-user experience?

  • What are some of to problems that RAID tries to resolve? What are some of the example use-cases?

  • What are some Windows/Linux tools to check if a disk’s performance is maxed out or spikes at certain time throughout the day?

Database

  • Your customer is complaining about the performance of their database server, how would you troubleshoot this?

  • Your company is running a website and expecting a surge in traffic coming into your database tier, what would you do in order to get it ready to cope with this workload?

  • You are planning to deploy a new e-commerce website. Would you choose a relational database, NoSQL or a combination or both for your database tier?

  • Your e-commerce website is using a commercial database that needs to be scaled and its licenses need to be renewed. You are tasked to optimize costs for this database tier. Which approach would you follow?

  • You have to design a reporting solution for your company, would you create reports from the production databases or would you deploy any additional components in your infrastructure?

  • You need to keep your session state information out of your webservers in your e-commerce website. Where would you store this information?

  • You are asked to design a disaster recovery strategy for the database tier of your e-commerce website. Due to compliance reasons, RPO has to be 15 minutes and RTO 1 hour. How would your strategy meet these requirements?

Big Data

  • Are you familiar with MapReduce? Can you go through the process of a MapReduce Job in simple terms?

  • What are the three main components in Hadoop?

DevOps

  • What are some of the goals and outcomes teams like to get from adopting DevOps methodologies?

  • If you would start today, how would you manage continuous development and releases for a e-commerce web application?

  • What would you focus on if you need to help a customer to decide between a scripting language and a compiled language?

  • How would you ensure a web application stays accessible to users when deploying a new version (of the front-end)?

Security

  • What measures would you recommend to secure a traditional 3-tier e-commerce application?

  • You are responsible for a typical multi tier web architecture consisting of a load balancer tier, web tier, and database tier. What are some possible attack vectors that can be exploited and ways to guard against it?

  • Tell me about a DDoS, malware or cryptolocker event you’ve been involved in responding to. What actions did you take to resolve the issue, and what actions did you take to manage user expectations during the event? (If you don’t have direct experience than how would you react if you were a TAM and you get paged in the middle of the night by your customer who suspects they are either under a DDoS attack or a Security Exploitation. How you would you react to this particular scenario?)
