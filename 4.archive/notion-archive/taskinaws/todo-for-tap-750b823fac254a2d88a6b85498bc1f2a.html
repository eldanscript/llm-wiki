3.3 Technical Account Strategy (1 page max, Tech strategy should be tightly aligned through the doc)

Ad Astra Telecom plans a hybrid IT setup (30:70 Cloud) with Infosys handling partial outsourcing in 2024, incorporating on-premises, public cloud, and SaaS solutions. Ad Astra Telecom’ IT landscape is divided into Enterprise IT (CIO-managed) and Network IT (CTO-managed). Operating across six Wakandaan data centers, four have max-redundant direct connections (10GB each, totaling 12) to AWS cloud and Megaport. In 2021, AWS Control Tower expanded to include multi-org (60 AWS accounts, 8 existing organizations, and 2 more planned) with a minimum-security baseline. Additionally, the Cost Optimization score is currently 65 with a goal to increase this to 90 by H2 FY24 through Graviton4 and managed service adoption. The migration and modernization journey faced challenges in 2022 due to PCCW’s incompetence, leading to their termination. Infosys, the new IT vendor, will reassess app migration/modernization from February 2024 with a phased approach in collaboration with AWS. The CIO prioritizes a SAAS-first strategy due to a lean IT team. In 2023, core applications, including Matrixx and Amazon Workspaces, moved successfully through PCCW. The C360 data analytics awarded in Dec 23 will deploy on AWS through TechM.

3.3.1 Customer Technical Landscape

AWS has been engaged with Ad Astra Telecom Enterprise IT and Infosys to identify key applications that can be moved to AWS Cloud. Ad Astra Telecom Network, the lower layers, including OSS and Network, began its transformation journey with the new CTO on board in June 22. We have successfully identified potential opportunities and work is underway to see these to fruition. For the Analytics and Customer 360 platform, AWS had two significant wins (See Appendix: Customer Technical Landscape; Error! Reference source not found.).

3.3.2 AWS Strategic Services

Leveraging AWS Strategic Services, we transform Ad Astra Telecom’s IT landscape, centralizing data for empowered decision-making, integrating AI/ML (GenAI) for enhanced analytics and personalized experiences. Adoption of AWS Compute, including Graviton, Trainium, and Inferentia, optimizes efficiency and cuts operational costs. Simultaneously, AWS S3 One Zone ensures scalable and durable data retrieval.

3.3.3 Industry Solutions: AWS and Partners

In collaboration with AWS, Amdocs will transform Ad Astra Telecom to modernize OSS/BSS, enhancing operational efficiency and delivering superior customer experiences in the telecommunications landscape. This partnership ensures a seamless integration of cutting-edge technologies for a future-proof OSS/BSS transformation. [Appendices (i) Application & Infrastructure overview and (ii) Key AWS Services]

3.3.4 Data Strategy

In crafting a robust AWS data strategy, our primary focus is on ensuring seamless accessibility, scalability, and security of Ad Astra Telecom’s data assets. Our approach involves implementing a data lake architecture to aggregate and store diverse datasets, fostering a centralized and unified data lake repository on S3. AWS Glue will play a pivotal role in automating data preparation, enabling efficient ETL processes for streamlined analytics. To enhance data processing speed and cost-effectiveness, we will leverage Amazon Redshift for high-performance data warehousing. The incorporation of AWS Lambda for serverless computing will enable real-time data processing, ensuring timely insights. This holistic strategy aims to optimize data management, analytics, and security, thereby empowering our organization to make data-driven decisions with confidence and agility.

3.3.5 Security Strategy

Ad Astra Telecom, under Wakanda’s regulation, faces security challenges in managing AWS workloads amid rapid business growth mainly due to lack of resources with cloud security knowledge. Key customer’s security goals for 2024 include 1/ operationalize cloud security and improve internal capabilities, and 2/ improve cloud security posture and increase automation. ProServ is in advanced talks with CISO Maurice Zurita to revamp the security operating model, impacting internal processes, personnel, and tools. Q1 will witness the launch of a pivotal project, accompanied by quarterly security workshops delivered by ProServ and Security SA teams to enhance knowledge transfer and bolster the cloud security posture (60% Security Health Score as of Dec 2023). This strategic engagement provides a unique opportunity to establish mindshare within Ad Astra Telecom’s leadership, uncovering potential collaborative security opportunities. Details can be found in the Appendix Security.
