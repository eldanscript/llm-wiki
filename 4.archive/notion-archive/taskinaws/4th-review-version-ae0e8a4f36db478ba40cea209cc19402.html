Head of Account Planning

HYBE is Korea’s no.1 entertainment company who has first achieved over $1B revenue among the domestic label companies. With the brand artists like BTS, New Jeans, and LE SSERAFIM, HYBE has become the global entertainment label beyond the Korea market. HYBE is striving to be the global music-based lifestyle entertainment company utilizing emerging technology with the following 

strategies
. 1/ Artist & Music IP diversification with the multi-label strategy and investments on the global labels, 2/ invest & acquire in new technologies and companies such as game(Superb), AI/ML(Supertone), and VFX(Giant Step) which can create added value on utilizing artists and music intellectual properties (IPs), 3/ collaborate with other players in the market to adopt their specialties to its contents (i.e. NFT – Dunamu, Fandom Community – Kakao & SM, Co, Contents Generation – Pinkfong). In order to align with the customer’s strategy, the account team will focus on the following 

initiatives
; 1/ Realize the DCM 2.0 (Digital Contents Management) opportunity, an end-to-end contents supply chain platform from creation, store, edit to the management on cloud. Thus, benefit the customer with standardized and steady contents production workstation and collaboration infrastructure across its many multi-labels, 2/ provide an agile infrastructure with migration which is easy to be expandable to go global and build a standardized DevOps environment with container based modernization to follow up with the company expansion, 3/ drive AI collaboration initiatives on HYBE’s focused business areas (i.e. Generative AI-driven contents creation & VFX, AI/ML data analytics to gain insights in contents creation decision making & consumer data analytics) to dive deeper with the customer’s business and create a lighthouse case in media AI. The team will tie these initiatives up on the HYBE group executive level with the 

think big
 of Pan-Amazon partnership through global EBC and SCA. Therefore, our key asks are 1/ An executive Sponsorship from both of Korea and the APJ level to discuss on the Pan-Amazon Strategy and Initiatives, 2/ a strategic investment fund of 1Mfortrainingresourcesonon−prem(NVIDIAA100–2servers)torealizeall−inmigrationofSupertone,3/ProServefundingof25K (50:50 investment) to build DCM2.0 (Digital Contents Management, CMS on cloud) which will bring additional opportunity of 4PB archive migration and cloud editing. With targeting to position AWS as both technical and business partner for HYBE to achieve the key strategy, we set a bold goal to achieve $30M revenue generation by 2025.
