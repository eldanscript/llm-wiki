Biz Reference

LoB - Content Production

LOB personas:

  • Head of production

  • Chief information officer (CIO)

  • Chief financial officer (CFO)

Business goals:

  • Produce quality content more efficiently

  • Capitalize on the best possible talent without geographic constraints

  • Increase creative and remote collaboration

  • Give artists more time to create and reduce time building out workstations

Business challenges:

  • Unprecedented audience demand for new content; need to accelerate speed of deployment

  • Need cloud content production capabilities to quickly set up remote artist
    workstations, editorial workflows, collaboration platforms, and
    rendering

  • Large file sizes make remote collaboration challenging

  • Lack of flexibility in traditional on-premises configurations

  • Content egress fees for on-premises workloads when rest of supply chain is in cloud

LoB - Data Science & Analytics

LOB personas:

  • Chief data officer (CDO)

  • Chief technology officer (CTO)

  • Chief information officer (CIO)

Business goals:

  • Accelerate time to market

  • Increase value of content repository

  • Speed up content discovery and increase audience engagement

  • Better understand and monetize audiences who use multiple devices across their portfolio of products

Business challenges:

  • Need to understand what drives audience engagement

  • Need to analyze billions of audience events per day and leverage machine
    learning to segment audiences, forecast inventory, perform contextual
    analysis, and create personalized experiences

  • Complex to search, re-purpose, and monetize archive

Media Supply Chain & Archive

LOB personas:

  • SVP of supply chain

  • Chief technology officer (CTO)

  • Chief information officer (CIO)

Business goals:

  • Reduce costs associated with additional egress and transport fees

  • Improve speed and security when transferring assets

  • Reduce manual efforts and time to market

Business challenges:

  • Decades-old video archives stored in tape libraries and on-premises systems are costly to access, and susceptible to data loss

  • Frequent transfer of media content is complex

  • Develop deeper data insights and improve visibility of internal content supply chain and ecosystem

  • Globalization requires localization at scale

Broadcast

LOB personas:

  • Head of distribution engineering

  • Chief technology officer (CTO)

  • Chief information officer (CIO)

  • Chief financial officer (CFO)

Business goals:

  • Reduce operating costs

  • Enable regional advertising vs. one-to-all over satellite

  • Fully managed service with service level agreements (SLAs) vs. building in-house

Business challenges:

  • Audience fragmentation: audiences now consume broadcast content on multiple
    devices and platforms making tracking and measuring of viewer behavior
    more challenging

  • Digital disruption: the rise in digital streaming services has caused a decline in viewership

  • Need to innovate more quickly

  • Looking for global scale with regionalization capabilities

D2C & Streaming

LOB personas:

  • Head of distribution engineering

  • Head of product

  • Chief information officer (CIO)

  • Chief technology officer (CTO)

  • Chief data officer (CDO)

Business goals:

  • Reduce operating costs

  • Enable regional advertising vs. one-to-all over satellite

  • Fully managed service with SLAs vs. building in-house

Business challenges:

  • Increasingly crowded and competitive direct-to-consumer marketplace

  • Need to better monetize existing content or find new monetization models

  • Looking for global scale with regionalization capabilities

  • Grow audience and improve audience engagement metrics
