LG Group -2022

#19. Technical account plan

Technical Landscape

LG Group-wise technical landscape can be described with multiple aspect. 1/The selected companies specific industry domain such as LGE from Electronics group, LGU from Service group and LGES from chemical group from LG group business category. 2/Maturity Level of Cloud adoption, 3/Service Trends, and 4/SA Engagement. To be more analytical, we describe technical point of view with 2 perspective. One is maturity for cloud adoption and its service trend by company and the other is Technical Agenda and SA engagement by Organization. Lesson & Learnt from the previous year is appended at the end of technical landscape.

Maturity for cloud adoption & AWS Service trend:

LG Electronics (LGE)

is XXL-sized account. The maturity level of cloud adoption of LGE is silo and from LGE we can analyze CIO and CTO separately. In the case of the CIO department, the maturity level is at the Migration stage and owns ERP, MES, and SCM. It is expected that a large-scale migration will be inevitable depending on the group cloud migration strategy but the mass migration decision of CIO is sluggish. The new business workload started since 2019, born in the cloud: the enterprise data lake for ERP/SCM data with GCP BiqQuery, and Call Center Migration with Connect, LGe.com is building the next generation with AWS. CIO is highly dependent in LGCNS for system integration and operation. On the contrary CTO is on the edge of transition from modernization to reinvention stage. We have collaborated with CTO through ThinQ/SDP as connected product platform of LGE appliances and smart TVs. The LGE CTO is collaborating with AWS on the connected platform and device data analysis platform. IoT usage grew 125% YoY aligned with LGE’s Appliances sales growth which connected to thinQ platform using AWS IoT/Lamda and API GW. As thinQ experiences various events such as AWS service incident, geo-special event due to global due to global market expansion, and traffic surge due to COVID-19 by growing number of connected devices. Collaboration on device security, management and pattern analysis, and cost-effective architectures is increasing. ThinQ team started to use device defender and device management. The device data analytics platform makes YoY growth by Redshift(196%), QuickSight(1380%), Athena(493%), and OpenSearch(130%). Recently we started assessment modernization requirements for LG Smart TV Service Delivery Platform with cloud platform lab. The workload has been 320KMRRin2021andincreasedto719K MRR in 2022, with 50% of the usage is EC2.

LG Uplus (LGU)

can be defined as a modernization stage but different progress by organization. CIO/CTO plan the new service as toward cloud native and is gradually re-platforming the existing on-prem workload toward cloud. The LGU CIO collaborates with AWS for Telco BSS through LGCNS suggesting hybrid architecture because of tight dependency on Oracle RAC, which should be remained in on-prem

.

Cloud migrated workload of BSS adopt Aurora MySQL as data layer, data migration with DMS and MSA with EKS based architecture and stream analysis with MSK(1068% growth). CTO collaborate with AWS mostly for media-related business, which is a core workload, with growing momentum. Media center is under re-platform with Elemetal services and Cloud Front. The CNO, network business sector is currently in the Project stage in the maturity of cloud adoption, and with the recent spread of DISH cases, started collaboration with AWS in the 5G area by requesting PoC for 5G Core DR. While the CIO is not free from LGCNS’s technical advice in the process of cloud adoption, the CTO is technologically independent from LGCNS and is rapidly preparing for the Reinvention level.

LG Energy Solutions (LGES)

is in the Project level in cloud adoption maturity. After spun off from LG Chemical, LGES is actively promoting the introduction of cloud for digital transformation. As a traditional manufacturing company, LGES tends to be passive in migrating on-prem workload to cloud as an information security policy. Digital transformation business drive collaboration with AWS, related to ML for Smart Factory usecase. LGES CIO absolutely dependent on LGCNS in the IT area. Currently PoC is in-progress with MLSL and collaborate on MLOps with the SageMaker usage gradually grows by 100% YoY.

LG AI Resaerch (LGAIR)

is core ML intelligence of LG-group driving advanced ML research for the synergy of sister companies in LG group. Recently decided SageMaker for giant-model training platform and reserved $2.5M yearly usage including FSx for Lustre.

(More details on service trends, please find “Service Trend by account”)

Challenges from the previous year:

Issue-1: Partner capability readiness of LGCNS for LG group-in business

1.1/LGU Enterprise Data Analytics Platform PoC win but business loss against GCP BigQuery through LGCNS political decision: The partner had lack of AWS analytics & AI/ML services capability, instead, prefers in-house and 3rd party solutions so that impacted cases happened such as LGU’s enterprise data analytics platform selection. AWS Redshift & EMR solution lost to GCP BigQuery. Service complexity had a bad effect on user convenience, and the skill readiness of partner who could help with it, was weak.

1.2/LGU BSS monolithic to MSA, LGCNS was lack of MSA experiences, they setup irreasonable data migration plan causing Auroa performance degradation such as CDC of daily batch and makes (100M TPS) and challenged max IOPS of Aurora. During development phase, monolithic architecture to implement BBS application, and lost LGU’s trust and requested AWS to assign 4 SAs on-site to work as a project member for months to solve issues.

1.3/LGCNS as partner : not working for group-in business but positioning as customer: Request answering about frequent tech questions to with insufficient context by hiding business background. When request end-user usecase, the answer is business confidential. It make ambiguous and demanding situation to SAs who worked with LGCNS and it increase account team churn. for SA & TAM community.

Issue-2: Service Feature Readiness / Operational excellence

2.1/Connect: Number porting issues at LGE call center caused customer service disruption almost for 10 hours and account team met holiday emergency case during the end of year 2021, The root cause was the lack of maturity of the manual process related to number porting between carriers.

2.2/Connect: Customer’s expectation about contact lens is higher than current readiness. The comprehend & transcribe features require near real-time response in the customer’s soft-phone usecase.

2.3/Connect: Customer critical requirement and Service Region has gap: India region is not in plan in 1-2 years.

2.4/Storage: hundreds of PB scale image archiving business lost to GCP of lack of feature readiness which requires instant retrieval of archived data with cost effectiveness. If Glacier Instant Retrieval got ready earlier it was better situation.

Issue-3: Frequent Service Event

3.1/ThinQ, the connected product back-end platform for LGE devices got impacted by 5 service incidents including IoT core(2) & Lamda(3) and 7 region impacts, ICN(2), PDX(3), and IRD(2). It was directly impacted LGE Customer’s satisfaction. The RCA type is concluded to issues during deploy(2) and from defect(3).

Issue-4: Service Capacity shortage

4.1/ P4 type instance was difficult to reservation for customer’s large scale model training. After all, account team and SageMaker team’s special care worked and signed PPA, but need more effective mechanism to reserve instances if the resource is short.

4.2/P3 type instance shortage impacted ML training productivity at LGE AI lab and live streaming & broadcasting service of LGU+ in case of infrequently requirement of on-demand high volume capacity of instance.
