Q: Please provide a justification for high spend including a detailed description of use case, rationale for amount of resources, size and length of time along with expected outcomes/deliverables

A: My customer, DreamUs Company plans to build image asset generation service for internal design and marketing tema, utilizing Generative AI model. The customer hopes to generate images with their design identity, so they need to fine-tune a text-to-image model of Titan Image Generator. To help and guide to develop custom model, I have tested a fine-tuning features of Titan Image Generator model in Amazon Bedrock. From 4/2 to 4/20, I have built ten training jobs using around a thousand of images to optimize hyperparameter and data labeling. During image training, 5M of images are processed and the cost is calculated as $0.005 (cost per image) * 5M (count of images) = $25K. 

Q: Have the resources been stopped/terminated?
