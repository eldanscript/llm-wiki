2026년 6월 06일40 min read

Customer Engagements & Business Activities

  • HYBE DCM 구축 (ARR $360K) : HYBE is migrating their on-prem Digital Content Management (DCM) system with 8PB content to AWS, targeting November 2025 launch. He supported their migration by providing OpenSearch architecture guidance for metadata indexing and search capabilities, where PoC validation demonstrated 5x performance improvement and led to OpenSearch adoption. HYBE plans to enhance the system with context-based search and automated artist face indexing using Media2Cloud. This implementation establishes new reference for content management system.

  • Supertone GPU for Voice AI Serving : Supertone adapted Inf2 instances and SageMaker inference endpoints for their light TTS model serving. This migration aimed to improve scalability and cost efficiency. He supported customer by designing SageMaker-based serving architecture with EKS and serverless integration, while guiding Inf2 instance migration by providing workarounds for unsupported Neuron SDK features. This achieved significant performance improvements and enabled their US region expansion.

  • HYBE Amazon Q Developer Pro Adoption : HYBE and HYBE IM launched subscriptions of Amazon Q Developer Pro for users across Infra Operation and SRE teams. He supported their adoption through demonstrations and practical use cases, resulting in team-specific adaption: HYBE DevOps team integrated it with VSCode for CI/CD pipeline management, while HYBE IM’s SRE team leveraged CLI for EKS operations. The implementation serves as a reference for expanding adoption to other HYBE affiliates, demonstrating its value as a core tool for AWS operations.

  • YG Entertainment Backup with S3 : YG Entertainment decided to migrate 3PB content to S3 Glacier, initiating with a 400TB Snowball transfer. He supported this transformation by providing migration strategy and cost analysis with Storage Specialists. This transition enables advanced content management and utilization through AI-powered metadata generation.

  • HYBE CMS Migration (DCM 2.0) : HYBE opted to migrate their DCM and 5PB contents to the cloud. To facilitate this project decision, he continuously shared case studies and business value of cloud-based content workflows like Amazon Studio, while also proposing and supporting the implementation of a Direct Connect networking architecture that significantly reduced costs for data transfer (65% down) with Network SSA@sungro. The project scope expanded to include cloud-based production and AI-powered services for content search, review, filtering and localization.

  • HYBE OnHYBE Modernization : HYBE modernized OnHYBE, an internal back-office service, by transforming features into microservices and re-platforming the service, enhancing functionality and user experience for employees. He led this transformation by conducting 4 customized AWS Immersion Days and collaborating with the Prototyping team@seyong to support the DevAx program, which upskilled a development team in AWS technologies, enabling cloud capabilities. This initiative modernized OnHYBE and set the stage for broader AWS adoption at HYBE, paving the way for future innovations like AI chatbots.

  • HYBE Internal System Search using GenAI : HYBE is set to launch an AI-powered HR chatbot using Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Base in mid-December. He led the project by conducting Amazon Bedrock Immersion Days, proposing a RAG architecture, and providing templates for Slack integration. During the two-month PoC, he provided regular assistance, reducing response latency by 10 seconds. Close collaboration with the development team resulted in a practical service that outperformed alternatives from other CSP. Based on this project, the development team plans to expand towards enhancing and personalizing HYBE’s work systems with AWS.

  • Weverse Company Media Streaming on AWS : Weverse Company expanded its media streaming capabilities using AWS Elemental Media services, migrating part of the workload from Naver Cloud and implementing AWS as a backup for live stream events. He supported the Live Backend team in implementing live and VoD streaming using AWS Elemental MediaLive and MediaConvert, focusing on cost-effectiveness and resilience. Building on the trust established through successful AWS service implementation, Weverse is now exploring advanced AI-based AWS services, such as video upscaling and live translation, to further enhance their media offerings and user experience.

  • HYBE HYBE Supertone Voice AI Serving Platform: S: HYBE intend to build voice AI serving platform with various models of voice separation and synthesis to the world-wide creators and build AI serving platform considering scalability, cost-effectiveness and global service availability. They plan to migrate inferencing platform of dev env from GCP to AWS and build prod environment to launch AI serving platform on AWS A: He delivered quick-win sessions of AWS and EKS features for customer’s fast enablement with AWS (Mar2023) and Until June2023, he guided to build kubernetes clusters on Amazon EKS and delivered the best practices of security, authentication, auto scaling, instance selection, cost-effective selection and introduced the inferentia 2 and trainium instances for the inferencing acceleration and fine-tuning workloads and provided the neuron SDK with beta release for customer’s fast adoption. R: Customer succeed in migration with Amazon EKS, Amazon ECR, Amazon S3, Amazon EFS and building voice AI serving platform as planned with Midnatt(artist) adoption as the first music project.
