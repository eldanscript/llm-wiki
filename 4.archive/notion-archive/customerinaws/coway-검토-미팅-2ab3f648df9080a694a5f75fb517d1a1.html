Next

The AWS Streaming Services team presented comprehensive roadmap updates and new capabilities across their portfolio to field teams and customers. The session focused heavily on MSK Express Brokers as a game-changing offering, along with significant updates to Kinesis Data Streams, Flink, and Firehose. Major announcements included new Kafka version support, enhanced security features, and upcoming automation capabilities that will reduce operational overhead for customers.

Key Product Updates

MSK Express Brokers Performance and Features

MSK Express Brokers deliver up to 3x better sustained throughput compared to standard brokers, with the ability to scale up to 20x faster during partition rebalancing operations. In testing, Express Brokers completed rebalancing in minutes without customer throughput loss, compared to 175 minutes with standard brokers. The service now supports up to 5 million partitions per broker and provides 90% faster recovery times. Express Brokers eliminate storage management overhead entirely and implement Kafka best practices by default, making them the recommended starting point for new customer deployments.

Kafka Version and Security Enhancements

The team announced support for Kafka versions 3.7, 3.8, and upcoming 3.9 across MSK services. Customer-managed encryption keys (CMKs) are now supported, addressing a critical requirement for financial services and regulated industries. Apache Flink now supports versions up to 1.1.20 with in-place upgrades and rollback capabilities.

Upcoming Automation Features

Automatic partition rebalancing and heat management for MSK Express will launch before year-end, eliminating the need for manual partition management. Policy-based scaling is planned for next year. MSK Replicator will expand to support cross-account connectivity and migration capabilities from on-premises Kafka clusters to MSK.

Service-Specific Roadmaps

Kinesis Data Streams Enhancements

Upcoming features include support for large records up to 10 megabytes, on-demand warm throughput capacity, and lower on-demand pricing for committed usage customers. The service will increase EFO consumer limits to 100 per stream and add integration with additional AWS services. These capabilities are expected within 6-9 months.

Flink Development Direction

Flink will skip version 2.0 and move directly to version 2.1 to provide JDK 17 support. The team is developing a new configuration model supporting up to 37 configuration options and exploring disaggregated compute and storage architecture. Integration with SageMaker Unified Studio is planned for later next year, along with expanded compute choices for customers.

Firehose Connectivity Expansion

Firehose recently added zero-ETL support and auto-scaling capabilities, with new destinations including Snowflake and Iceberg tables. Upcoming features include Splunk connectivity, increased partition limits, and HDFS destination connectors. The team is also working on better file size optimization for S3 and Iceberg table destinations.

Strategic Positioning and Customer Guidance

The team emphasized that Express Brokers should be the default recommendation for new MSK deployments unless customers have specific requirements for standard brokers. For migration scenarios, the focus should be on understanding whether customers need messaging services (SQS, Active MQ, RabbitMQ) or streaming services (MSK, Kinesis) based on their long-term architecture goals. The multiple service options are positioned as a feature providing customers with appropriate tools for different use cases rather than redundant offerings.

Decisions

  • Express Brokers should be the default recommendation for new MSK customers unless specific requirements dictate otherwise

  • Flink will skip version 2.0 and jump directly to version 2.1 for JDK 17 support

  • AWS will not invest in building extensive supported connector libraries, preferring open source community solutions

  • Topic APIs will be developed to make Kafka topics manageable as AWS resources with IAM permissions

Next Steps

  • Automatic partition rebalancing and heat management for MSK Express to launch before end of year

  • Cross-account connectivity support for MSK Replicator in development

  • Flink 2.1 support and new configuration model with 37 options to be delivered

  • Integration work with SageMaker Unified Studio team for streaming services

  • Follow up on customer lease protection requirements for MSK

  • Engage with customers interested in backup/snapshot capabilities for detailed requirements gathering
