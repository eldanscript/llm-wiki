  • 테스트 결과

  • multilingual model

  • small , tiny 모델의 경우 성능이 애매함

  • medium, large 모델은 한글 성능이 나쁘지 않음, 실사용에서는 large 모델을 사용해야 할 것 같음

  • eng-only model

  • small 모델부터 성능이 나쁘지 않음

  • inference time

  • Large Model 기준 40분 영상 처리에 RTX3080 Inference Time : 20min

  • 결과 분석

  • 영어, 한국어를 섞어 말하더라도 잘 분리해서 서술함

  • 일부 전문용어, 약어들도 잘 분류함 (ddos -> DDoS)

  • 한국어 저자가 있어서 그런지 한국어 transcribe도 잘해주는 것 같음

  • 클로바 노트와 같은 상용 서비스에 비해 성능이 좋다고 할 수 없으나 대안이 될 수 있을 것으로 보임

  • 영어 transcribe는 상당히 성능이 좋음

  • 영상 inference의 경우 파이썬 코드는 생각보다 inference time이 김

Custom Model

Custom Vocabulary

  • Custom Vocabularies do not re-train the model. It effectively goes alongside the general model, and when it is choosing the most likely words, based upon the phonemes heard and other words in the very recent stream, then the Custom Vocab overrides the built-in model output. This is partly why we say don’t use more than a few hundred words in the vocab, as you are literally override what Transcribe thinks it hears and you end up seeing “phantom” words. There is no feedback into the model here. If you want to re-train the model in any way then Custom Language Models is the only way.

Improve Transcribe Performance

  1. Use lossless audio (WAV, FLAC) and not MP3 if you can

  2. Keep the original sample rate - our 8Khz/16Khz are tuned for those, so don’t up-scale or down-scale

  3. If they have 2 speakers (such as a telephone call) try and get channel-separated audio, not mono; with mono you get lots of simple
    errors when speakers are talking over one another and you’ll miss entire words

  • pass any poorly transcribed words (low-confidence) to a human in the loop review, get them to update/maintain real mistakes into the vocab list, re-train the vocab every night or so
